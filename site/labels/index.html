Printer alignment

Moves everything on the label to make up for the printer. It applies to every template on this device.

Shift right
Shift down

How to set it:

  1. Download the alignment test and print it.
  2. Measure the gap between the printed border and the label edge on the left (L) and on the right (R).
  3. Add (R − L) ÷ 2 to "Shift right". Example: L = 0 mm, R = 3 mm → add 1.5 mm.
